Quick guide

How to play

  1. Look around the street view and hunt for clues: signs, road markings, driving side, landscape.
  2. Hover over the small map (bottom right) to enlarge it, then click to drop your pin.
  3. Press Guess before the timer runs out. The closer you are, the more points (max 5,000).
  4. Duos share one team pin. Multiplayer rooms hold up to 20 players.
